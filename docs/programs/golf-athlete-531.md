# Golf Athlete 5/3/1 — 12 weeks, 4 days/week (intermediate)

Strength that shows up on the tee. Wendler 5/3/1 run Leader → Leader → Anchor,
with the golf research built in: every session opens with mobility and
max-intent jumps or throws, trap-bar and single-leg work build ground force,
and a dedicated Golf Power day keeps rotational speed sharp.

**Lifts & TM increments:** Squat +10 · Bench +5 · Trap-Bar Deadlift +10 ·
Press +5 (applied after each completed wave). TM starts at 90% of e1RM from a
recent top set — the app computes it at onboarding.

## The week

| Day | Name | Backbone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lower | Trap-bar jumps 5×3 → Squat 5/3/1 → Trap-bar DL FSL → split squat, Nordic curls, Pallof |
| 2 | Upper | MB chest pass 3×5 → Bench 5/3/1 → Bench FSL → chins + rows (superset), face pulls, suitcase carry |
| 3 | Golf Power (30–40 min) | Rotational scoop toss 4×3/side, step-behind throw, broad jumps, lateral bounds, band chops, optional Zone 2 |
| 4 | Press + Arms | MB slam 3×5 → Press 5/3/1 → Press FSL → dips + pull-ups (superset), curls, cuff work |

Every lifting day opens with a 3-item mobility primer (90/90, open books, and
friends). Jumps/throws are ≤3 reps per set at maximum intent with full rest —
speed work, never cardio.

## The 12 weeks

| Weeks | Block | Main-lift sets (%TM) | Supplemental |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Leader 1 · 5s PRO | 65/75/85 → 70/80/90 → 75/85/95, all ×5, no AMRAP | 5×5 FSL |
| 4 | Deload | 40/50/60 ×5, accessories dropped | — |
| 5–7 | Leader 2 · 5s PRO | same waves, heavier TM | 5×5 FSL |
| 8 | Deload | 40/50/60 ×5 | — |
| 9–11 | Anchor · PR sets | 65/75/**85×5+** → 70/80/**90×3+** → 75/85/**95×1+**, AMRAPs capped at RPE 9 | 3×5 FSL |
| 12 | TM test | warm to **90% TM × 5** per lift; app proposes new TMs | light golf power day |

Week 10's lower day flips emphasis: trap-bar deadlift takes the 5/3/1 top sets
and squat takes the FSL volume, so the pull gets real top-end exposure in the
peak block.

## Rules the engine enforces

- **No grinders.** Anchor AMRAP sets carry an RPE 9 cap — always a rep in the
  tank. Leaders have no AMRAP at all.
- **TMs bump only after a completed wave** (all four days of the pre-deload
  week logged).
- **Round tomorrow?** Toggle the flag and heavy lower slots swap to fast,
  light alternates (banded hinge, glute bridge). Swapped work never advances
  the program state.
- **Skipped ≠ failed.** A slot with nothing logged drives no progression.
- Accessories run double progression (e.g. 3×8–12): hit the top on every set
  and the load bumps next time.

## Conditioning guidance (not tracked as program days)

Zone 2 treadmill 2–3×/week, 30–45 min, 1–4% incline, conversational pace.
Optional Norwegian 4×4 once a week, ≥24 h from a heavy lower day. 8–10k steps
daily — a walked 18 covers it.
