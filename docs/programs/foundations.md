# Foundations — 12 weeks, 3 days/week (beginner)

From new-to-barbells to genuinely strong. Two weeks of light technique work to
groove the barbell patterns without losing momentum from existing dumbbell
training, then ten weeks of GZCLP — the best-regarded beginner progression
there is. Stalls are planned rep-scheme changes, never failures.

## Weeks 1–2 · Technique (light on purpose)

Three full-body days that keep the familiar dumbbell work and add one barbell
pattern each day at bar-weight: squat practice (day 1), press practice
(day 2), trap-bar hinge practice (day 3). Everything else runs double
progression so the momentum never stops: goblet squats, DB bench, rows, RDLs,
hip thrusts, planks, and a first taste of med-ball rotational throws for golf.

## Weeks 3–12 · GZCLP

Four workout templates rotate across Mon/Wed/Fri (A1 → B1 → A2 → B2 → A1 …):

| Template | T1 (heavy focus) | T2 (support) | T3 (push the reps) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Squat | Bench | One-arm DB row, dead bugs |
| B1 | Press | Trap-bar deadlift | DB hip thrust, side planks |
| A2 | Bench | Squat | Band-assisted chin-ups, Pallof press |
| B2 | Trap-bar deadlift | Press | MB scoop toss (golf speed), band pull-aparts |

Every day opens with the same 3-item primer: cat-cow, 90/90 hip switches,
band lateral walks.

**T1 progression:** 5×3 (last set AMRAP) at the working load. Succeed → +10 lb
(squat/deadlift) or +5 lb (bench/press) next time. Miss → same load, new
scheme: 6×2, then 10×1, then a fresh start at 90% of your best that day.
The weight on the bar only ever moves forward or holds — the rep scheme
absorbs the stalls.

**T2 progression:** 3×10 → 3×8 → 3×6 through the same load, then up.

**T3 progression:** rep ranges (e.g. 3×10–15). Hit the top on every set and
the load bumps. AMRAP-quality effort, always ~2 reps in reserve.

## Milestones worth naming

- First full push-up (incline push-ups are the path)
- First unassisted chin-up (the band gets thinner until it disappears)
- Bodyweight on the trap bar for 5
- A plate (135) on the squat — most novices get there inside 12 weeks
- 45-second side planks each side

The app celebrates each PR by name when it happens. Misses are data. Trends
are the truth. Two to three sessions a week, every week, beats any perfect
week that doesn't repeat.
